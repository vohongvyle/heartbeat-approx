How this works

The model

A 1D CNN reads 0.7 s of ECG around each heartbeat plus two rhythm features (how early the beat came, how long the pause after it). It sorts beats into N normal, S supraventricular, V ventricular and F fusion, the AAMI classes.

The chip

Weights and activations are 8‑bit. Every one of the ~27,000 multiplications per beat is looked up in the table of the chosen EvoApproxLib circuit. Energy counts the multiplier plus an exact 16‑bit adder per operation, using 45 nm figures.

Compensation

Approximate multipliers lean low or high on average. Compensation measures that lean on training beats and folds it into each neuron's bias. It costs nothing in hardware, but it does not always help.

Why sensitivity

About 1 in 4 test beats is abnormal. Missing a ventricular beat matters more than a false alarm, so the page reports sensitivity per class and counts abnormal beats called normal.
